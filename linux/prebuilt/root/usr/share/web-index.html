ShaderEmu Web

A browser for pages as they are written: text in their own faces and sizes and in any script, pictures, tables, boxes side by side, forms that search. No scripts are run: a page that is nothing without them shows what it says to readers who have none.

In VRChat the addresses listed on this page open at a click, with their style sheets and most of their pictures; another address needs one step by hand. VRChat does not let a world open an address by itself, only those it was made with and one a visitor gives it. So when you follow a link to somewhere else, or type an address above, a button appears by the display: it opens a panel with the address to copy and a field to paste it into, and the page opens. That is a rule of VRChat, not a fault of this computer.
